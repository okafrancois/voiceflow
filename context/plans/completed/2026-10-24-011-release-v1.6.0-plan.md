## Verifier corrections and independent review

No published artifact was modified to satisfy these checks. Two failed local
verifier attempts are preserved:

1. `verification-attempt1-key-extraction.log`: an over-greedy regex included
   neighboring compiled text (`deep`) after the valid embedded public key.
   Normalizing the complete two-line key envelope fixed comparison; two
   behavioral regressions failed before the fix and all three extraction tests
   passed afterward. R4 independently passed nine helper tests, eight additional
   assertions and inspection of the actual old/new binary keys.
2. `verification-attempt2-dmg-container-contract.log`: requiring a separate DMG
   ticket did not match the observed packaging sequence. The corrected verifier
   records its absence explicitly, rejects all other stapler failures, and
   requires the signed container plus the contained application's ticket and
   Gatekeeper acceptance. New contract checks had three failures before the
   helper and all five passed afterward. All 14 verifier helper tests passed;
   log: `/tmp/voiceflow-release-v160-verifier-final-green.log`.

Earlier R2 found that a raw-Minisign fallback differed from the actual updater's
outer-base64 protocol. It was removed before final artifact verification; R3
independently passed six signature tests and 14 extra assertions. Initial
packaging review and the fixture-only startup correction had no remaining
findings. These helper tests are verification evidence, not product tests or a
substitute for the completed exact-source CI and downloaded-artifact checks.
